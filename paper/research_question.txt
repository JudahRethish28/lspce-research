Research Question:
Can automated LLM-based scope validation and rule-based PII 
detection prevent legally non-compliant forensic evidence 
release in multi-tenant cloud environments?

Null Hypothesis (H0):
Automated scope validation and PII detection produce no 
measurable improvement in precision, recall, or processing 
time compared to manual human review for preventing 
non-compliant evidence release.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1):
Automated scope validation achieves precision and recall 
greater than 0.80, and automated PII detection achieves 
F1 greater than 0.80, with significantly lower processing 
time than manual review.
